## 1. Sign in

![Tenant login](/help/tenant-faq-01-login.png)

1. Open **/login**
2. Select **Tenant** (not Admin)
3. Enter **mobile number** (as on your agreement) or email + **password**
4. Tap **Sign in as Tenant**

**Problems?** Ask the owner to create or reset your portal login under Admin → Tenants.

---

## 2. Pay rent (logged-in tenants)

1. Menu → **Pay rent**
2. Check **billing month** (e.g. August 2026)
3. Review **dues breakdown** (rent + parking + maintenance + electricity)
4. **Pay via UPI** — scan QR or open UPI app
5. Copy **UTR** from your bank app
6. Submit: amount, date, UTR, optional screenshot
7. Wait for owner approval → **Receipt** appears under Receipts

**Note:** Move-in month has no dues. Dues start the month after you moved in.

---

## 3. Pay without login

![Pay without login](/help/tenant-faq-02-public-pay.png)

1. Open **/pay** or tap **Pay without login** on the login page
2. Enter **flat number** (e.g. C201)
3. Enter **registered mobile**
4. Pay via UPI (you enter the amount yourself)
5. Submit **UTR** for owner confirmation

---

## 4. Receipts

1. Menu → **Receipts**
2. Open approved payment → **Download PDF**

---

## 5. Electricity

1. Menu → **Electricity** — view bills
2. Pay with monthly dues on **Pay rent** (not separately)

---

## 6. Maintenance

1. Menu → **Maintenance**
2. Title + description + priority → **Submit**

---

## 7. Move out

1. Menu → **Move / transfer**
2. Choose **Move out** → reason → Submit
3. Pay **final month** dues (rent + electricity may still apply)

---

## Monthly checklist

- [ ] Check **Home** for outstanding
- [ ] **Pay rent** → UPI → submit UTR
- [ ] Confirm **Receipt** after approval
- [ ] Use **Maintenance** for flat issues

## Contact

For payment issues, message the owner with: **flat number**, **month**, **amount**, **UTR**.
